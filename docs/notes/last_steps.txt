# Step 10 — Evaluate the models

Since your target is a percentage, this is a **regression problem**.

Use:

### MAE

Mean Absolute Error.

If:

```text
Actual = 90%
Predicted = 86%
```

error:

```text
4 percentage points
```

If your MAE is:

```text
4.2
```

you can explain:

> "On average, the model's prediction differs from actual final occupancy by about 4.2 percentage points."

Also use:

* RMSE
* R²

For your project, **MAE is particularly easy to explain to an interviewer.**

# Step 11 — Very important: split the data correctly

Don't randomly split snapshot rows without thinking.

Remember:

```text
T001 at 20 days
T001 at 15 days
T001 at 10 days
T001 at 5 days
```

are all from the **same trip**.

You don't want:

```text
Training:
T001 at 20 days

Testing:
T001 at 10 days
```

because the model has already seen information from the same trip.

Instead, split by **trip_id**.

For example:

```text
70% trips → training
15% trips → validation
15% trips → testing
```

This is very important for avoiding leakage.
